### Challenge
13

### Goal
Cryptography can be easy, do you know what ROT13 is?  
cvpbPGS{abg_gbb_onq_bs_n_ceboyrz}

### Walkthrough
ROT13
- Each letter in the alphabet is replaced by the letter 13 positions ahead
- If it goes past Z, it wraps around to the beginning
- It’s a type of Caesar cipher (shift = 13)

Command used:
```
echo "cvpbPGS{abg_gbb_onq_bs_n_ceboyrz}" | tr 'A-Za-z' 'N-ZA-Mn-za-m'
```
